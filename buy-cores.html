What Is a Nova Core?

A Symbol of Your Support

A Nova Core represents your support for the development of Project Nova, the flagship game being built within the Epic Nova universe.

Your support helps us develop character customization, player spaces, exploration, world-building systems, multiplayer social features, original artwork, environments, and the technology needed to bring the game to life.
