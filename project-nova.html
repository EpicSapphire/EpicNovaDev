Open-World Social RPG

A New World Built Around Creativity, Exploration, and Connection

Project Nova is an open-world social RPG where players will create and customize their own characters, build and personalize their spaces, explore a growing world, and connect with other players through shared adventures and social experiences.

The goal is to create a living online universe where players can express themselves, discover new places, collect items, take part in activities, meet other players, and create their own stories.

THE GAME VISION

What Is Project Nova?

Project Nova is being designed as an open-world social role-playing game centered on character customization, personal spaces, exploration, world-building, player expression, and in-game socialization.

Players will be able to create their own identity, customize their appearance, personalize their living spaces, explore shared environments, interact with other players, and choose how they want to experience the world.

THE NOVA ECONOMY

Nova Bits, Shards, and Cores

Project Nova is planned to include a layered digital economy built around three forms of Nova Energy.

Nova Bits

The base form of Nova Energy, intended for everyday activities, rewards, collectibles, and smaller in-game purchases.

Nova Shards

A rarer form of energy intended for more valuable items, customization options, progression, and special unlocks.

Nova Cores

The rarest form of Nova Energy and a symbol of support for the development of Epic Nova and Project Nova.

Planned Energy Conversion

1 Nova Core = 100 Nova Shards = 10,000 Nova Bits

The economy, conversion system, and future in-game uses are still in development and may change as the project evolves.
