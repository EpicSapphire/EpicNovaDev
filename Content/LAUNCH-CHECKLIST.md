# ⚡ Launch Philosophy

Epic Nova Version 1.0 is an **Early Access experience**.

The objective is not to launch every planned feature.

The objective is to create a polished, welcoming, and immersive introduction to the Epic Nova universe while laying the foundation for future systems such as Player Profiles, the Nova Core economy, community features, automated player wallets, and Project Nova integration.

Version 1.0 establishes the foundation.

Future updates will expand the universe.

Every new system, feature, character, game, and adventure should continue moving Epic Nova closer to becoming a living, connected gaming universe.
