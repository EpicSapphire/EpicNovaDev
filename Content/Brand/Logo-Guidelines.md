# EPIC NOVA LOGO GUIDELINES v1.0

## Official Brand Identity

---

# Purpose

The Epic Nova logo is the primary visual identity of the Epic Nova universe.

It should always represent adventure, gaming, creativity, and the futuristic spirit of the brand.

The logo should immediately communicate that visitors are entering a digital gaming universe.

---

# Official Motto

**Override Reality.**

---

# Logo Philosophy

The Epic Nova logo is designed to feel like:

• A futuristic game launcher

• A digital portal

• A neon HUD

• A symbol of adventure

It should never feel corporate or generic.

---

# Official Logo Components

The complete logo consists of:

• Nova Byte

• Epic Nova wordmark

• Override Reality slogan (optional depending on usage)

These elements together form the complete Epic Nova identity.

---

# Nova Byte

Nova Byte is not decoration.

She is an essential part of the logo.

She represents:

• Community

• Gaming

• Creativity

• Adventure

• The guide into the Epic Nova universe

Whenever possible, the complete logo should include Nova Byte.

---

# Official Nova Byte Reference

Always use the approved Nova Byte Character Bible.

Never redesign her face.

Never substitute another character.

Always preserve:

• Sapphire blue eyes

• Freckles

• Soft rounded face

• Magenta hair

• Blue highlights

• Cat-ear headset

• Chrome choker

• Epic Nova jacket

# Clear Space

Leave sufficient empty space around the logo.

Nothing should crowd Nova Byte or the Epic Nova wordmark.

Minimum spacing:

At least the height of the "E" in EPIC on every side.

---

# Minimum Size

Desktop

Approximately 300–700 pixels wide.

Mobile

Approximately 180–350 pixels wide.

Never reduce the logo so far that Nova Byte's face loses detail.
