# EPIC NOVA VISUAL IDENTITY MANUAL v1.0

> **Official Logo & Character Guide**

---

# Purpose

The Epic Nova Visual Identity is the primary representation of the Epic Nova universe.

Every logo, illustration, icon, profile picture, promotional image, and piece of official artwork should immediately communicate:

* Adventure
* Gaming
* Creativity
* Community
* Innovation

The goal is for every visitor to feel like they are stepping into a living gaming universe.

---

# Official Motto

> **Override Reality.**

---

# Visual Philosophy

Epic Nova should feel like:

* A futuristic game launcher
* A digital portal
* A cyberpunk HUD
* A sci-fi command center
* The beginning of an adventure

It should never feel corporate, generic, or lifeless.

---

# Official Logo Components

The complete Epic Nova logo consists of:

* Nova Byte
* Epic Nova wordmark
* Override Reality tagline *(optional depending on usage)*

Together these elements form the complete Epic Nova identity.

---

# Nova Byte

Nova Byte is **not decoration.**

She is the welcoming face of Epic Nova.

She represents:

* Community
* Gaming
* Creativity
* Adventure
* Curiosity
* Discovery

She is the guide into the Epic Nova universe.

Whenever possible, the complete logo should include Nova Byte.

---

# Character Composition

Nova Byte should never appear as a static decoration.

She is greeting the player.

Whenever composition allows:

* Right hand reaching toward the viewer in a welcoming gesture.
* Left hand naturally holding a game controller.
* Relaxed, open posture.
* Friendly eye contact.
* Warm, genuine smile.

The viewer should feel personally welcomed into Epic Nova.

She should feel like she is saying:

> **"Come on. Let's go on an adventure."**

—not posing for a photograph.

---

# Official Nova Byte Reference

Always use the approved **Nova Byte Character Bible**.

Never redesign her facial identity.

Never substitute another character.

Always preserve:

* Bright sapphire blue eyes
* Visible freckles across the cheeks and nose
* Soft rounded cheeks
* Rounded jawline
* Soft rounded chin
* Small, softly defined nose
* Small natural lips
* Warm, friendly smile
* Mature young-adult appearance (approximately mid-20s)
* Cute, approachable confidence
* Long wavy magenta hair
* Electric-blue highlights
* Cat-ear gaming headset
* Chrome holographic choker
* Epic Nova techwear jacket

Nova Byte should always look like the same person.

---

# Facial Identity Priority

Nova Byte's face is the single most recognizable element of the Epic Nova brand.

If an artistic decision improves the artwork but weakens Nova Byte's recognizable face...

**The artwork should be revised.**

Facial identity always takes priority over:

* Clothing
* Backgrounds
* Lighting
* Visual effects
* Dynamic poses

Players should recognize Nova Byte immediately—even in a small thumbnail.

---

# Visual Identity Test

Before approving any new logo or official artwork, ask:

1. Is Nova Byte immediately recognizable?
2. Does the artwork feel welcoming?
3. Does it communicate adventure?
4. Does it match the Character Bible?
5. Does it reinforce the Epic Nova Brand Bible?
6. Would a player instantly recognize it as Epic Nova?
7. Does it make someone want to learn more?

If the answer is **yes** to all seven questions...

The artwork is ready.
